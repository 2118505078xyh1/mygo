## Changes in behavior

No breaking changes to the Go API. A few changes apps and builds may notice:

- **A launch waits for an instance that is shutting down.** With `RequestSingleInstanceLock`, the running instance now holds the lock until its process exits, so a `Relaunch`, an update's relaunch or a quick reopen waits for the old process to finish instead of running beside it. Two launches a few milliseconds apart no longer both run. (#1909, reported by @lizhongyue248 in #1906, with @yudongyouqing)
- **Notarized builds submit twice.** With `macos.notarize`, `mygo build` notarizes and staples the app before it goes into the disk image, then the image as before, which adds a few minutes. The app users drag to `/Applications` now passes Gatekeeper offline. (#1898, @PythonShe)

## Highlights

- **Every finger on a touchscreen.** `Element.Touches()` returns each finger that is down on an element, with an ID that lasts from landing to lifting, where it landed, its phase and its pressure, so a whiteboard on a large touchscreen can recognize its own gestures, such as a four-finger swipe to turn the page or a five-finger tap to clear it. `Touch.Width` and `Height` give the size of each contact, so the same whiteboard can erase with a palm and draw with a finger. Works on Windows and in browsers; Linux reports fingers but no contact size. The `Tester` gets `TouchDown`, `TouchMove`, `TouchUp` and `TouchCancel`. Apps that never call `Touches` don't link any of it. (#1884, #1907, requested by @chenxiao1990 in #1879)
- **Menus that stay open, with colored badges, on macOS.** `MenuItem.KeepOpen` keeps a menu open when one of its items is chosen, so a menu bar proxy app can let the user try nodes one after another, or start a latency test, without the submenu closing each time. `BadgeShape`, `BadgeColor` and `BadgeTextColor` draw a badge on a square, rounded or pill background, so latencies can be colored by how good they are, and the setters update them in place. Other platforms show the items and badges as before. (#741, #1913, @Sakurasan)
- **Cancel a call from the page.** A bound Go method can be called with an `AbortSignal`, as `fetch` is: `await Notes.add.with({ signal })("Groceries")`. Aborting rejects the promise right away and cancels the method's `context.Context`, so a search the user typed past or a long command they stopped ends in Go too. `AbortSignal.timeout` works as a time limit, and existing calls are unchanged. (#1781, @joei4cm, requested by @echoVic in #1759)

## New

### Native UI
- `ListState.MoveAnimation` makes rows glide to their places when rows above or around them come, go or move, such as a deleted item or a commit list rewritten by a reset, instead of jumping there in one frame. It respects reduced motion. (#1904, @PythonShe)
- `Element.PopoverMenu` takes `PopoverMenuOptions` with `Panel` and `Item`, as `PopoverMenuButton` does, so right-click menus can open with the same panel style and animation as the app's ⋯ menus. (#1889, requested by @tsugumi42 in #1885)
- A translucent web view over a transparent window fades with its element, such as a peek flyout's page fading in over a window left live behind it. (#1793, @joei4cm, requested by @ourongxing in #1026)

### The app
- `NowPlayingInfo.Artists` names each artist of a track. On Linux, media controls list them one by one; macOS, Windows and browsers show them joined. On Linux, the media controls can also bring the player's window to the front. (#1924, requested by @Shizuku11451 in #1923)
- On macOS, `TrayOptions.MonospacedTitle` keeps a tray title of a fixed number of characters at one width, so a network speed such as `↓999M ↑999M` doesn't shift the icons beside it as its units change. (#1882, @pseudoyu)

## Fixes

### Windows
- An app whose process an input method kept alive at exit, such as with Doubao IME, now exits, so a `Relaunch` can replace its executable. (#1897, reported by @mistquay in #1896)
- Iframes from another host of a custom scheme load their scripts, styles and fetches. (#1914, reported by @mytlx in #1912)
- `mygo dev` rebuilds while the app runs a background service from its resources, and copies only resources that changed. (#1908, reported by @chenxiao1990 in #1905)

### Linux
- Under XWayland, a native menu that opens under the pointer stays open. (#1893, @ACAne0320)
- On GNOME, the dialog that confirms global shortcuts shows over the app's window under X11 and XWayland. (#1894, @ACAne0320)
- On GNOME, the file manager, browser or app that `Shell.ShowItemInFolder`, `OpenPath` or `OpenExternal` opens comes to the front. (#1895, @ACAne0320)

### Video
- On macOS, `plugins/render` gives every frame of a video its exact duration at any frame rate, so a 29.97 or 59.94 video plays at its own rate. (#1883, @pseudoyu)
